### Discord time zone navigation bot (aka Lapis)

Initially this bot was created for personal use on my discord server, it can help you to see your time and time in a different cities/countries.

For Instance, if your friends are in different parts of the globe now and you want to plan a gaming session, so you want to know whether your friends can play right now or no

Pretty customizable, you can add multiples time zones in the list.
